# Product Direction Notes

## Social Layer North Star

The app is a workout tracker first. Social features should make logged training more useful, more motivating, or easier to reuse. Avoid turning the product into a generic community feed.

Social features should attach to one of these objects:

- A workout
- A program
- A trainer
- An achievement

## Current Direction

Focus on a lightweight social layer instead of expanding groups as the main community bet.

Core social layer:

- Search users
- Add friends or follow users
- Share completed workouts
- View friends' shared workout history
- Copy or save shared workouts
- Keep privacy simple: private, friends, public

Trainer layer:

- Trainer profiles
- Follow trainers
- Trainer-published workouts and programs
- Save workouts from trainers
- Trainer attribution when a workout is reused

Outbound sharing:

- Instagram Story workout recap cards
- PR, streak, volume, distance, and program-progress cards
- Trainer attribution on shared cards when relevant

Delay until the behavior is proven:

- Broad groups
- Challenges
- Generic feed posts
- Comments
- Direct messages
- Group chat
- Complex moderation

## Product Boundary

Track your training. Share what you are proud of. Discover workouts from people worth following.

The feed, if added, should be workout-native: friends' completed workouts, milestones, trainer-published workouts, and reusable programs. It should not become open-ended lifestyle posting.

## AI Workout Direction

Generated workouts and plans should use the whole user context:

- Profile goals, sports, injuries, limitations, age, height, weight, and gender
- Equipment and session duration
- Recent training history and muscle recency
- Today's check-in: mood, sleep, and pain
- Check-in history and pre/post-workout mood patterns
- Recent adherence and training frequency
- Current program structure so new workouts complement rather than duplicate it

Daily generation should adapt the session for readiness. Plan generation should build around the user's real schedule, sport load, and recovery needs.
