Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see them in plain text).
  • Profile information you provide: username, display name, date of birth/age, gender, body weight, height, training goal, sports, a short "about" bio, and an optional profile photo.
  • Workout & training data: your workouts, exercises, logged sets (reps, weight, duration, distance), workout notes, and personal records.
  • Wellbeing check-ins: optional mood, perceived effort (RPE), and free-text notes that may include mentions of sleep, fatigue, pain, or injury.
  • Preferences & settings: units, theme, equipment, and similar app settings.
  • Social data: friend connections and workouts you choose to share with other users.

We do not collect precise location, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core app: storing your workouts and syncing them across your devices.
  • To generate AI-assisted features you request (workout suggestions and coaching insights).
  • To operate optional social features (friends and shared workouts).
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your health or training data for advertising.

AI features & third parties

Session includes AI features (AI Coach, workout generator, "Generate Today"). When they run, relevant training context — such as your recent workouts, goal, and wellbeing check-ins — is sent to our AI provider, OpenAI, to produce a response. Some of this happens automatically (for example, the AI Coach generates fresh insights about once per day when you open the app), and some happens when you tap an AI feature. AI output is informational only (see our Terms).

You can turn AI features off at any time in Settings → AI Features. When AI is off, no training data is sent to the AI provider, automatically or otherwise.

We rely on the following service providers ("subprocessors") to run the app:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage (hosts your account and app data).
  • OpenAI — powers AI coaching and workout generation features.
  • [HOSTING_PROVIDER] — serves the application.

Data storage, retention & backups

Your data is stored with Supabase and retained until you delete it or your account. To protect against data loss, we keep a rolling history of recent snapshots of your app data (up to the most recent 50 versions), which are removed when your account is deleted.

Your rights & choices

  • Access & export: you can export your data as JSON or CSV from Settings at any time.
  • Correction: you can edit your profile and training data in the app.
  • Deletion: you can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Data → Delete Account, or by following our account deletion instructions.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (access, portability, deletion, objection). Contact us to exercise them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your data is restricted by row-level security so that you can only access your own records. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.
