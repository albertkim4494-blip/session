Delete your Session account

You can permanently delete your Session account and all associated data at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete from within the app (recommended)

  1. Open Session and sign in.
  2. Open Settings (your profile) → Data.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

A backup of your data is exported to your device first, in case you want to keep a copy.

Can't access the app?

If you can no longer sign in, email [SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the address associated with your account and request deletion. We will verify your identity and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes: your profile, all workouts and logged history, wellbeing check-ins and notes, preferences, friend connections and shared workouts, uploaded images, data-recovery snapshots, and your login credentials. None of this data is retained after deletion, except where we are required to keep limited records by law.
